# Changelog

## 1.4.0
- New: AmmoBuddy is now available in **English, German, Spanish and French**. The language follows your game client.
- Item names, quest titles, zone names and reputation standings are shown in your game's language.

## 1.3.1
- Fixed: vendor pins were hidden under the map art of **OpenMap**. They now show above it, and on OpenMap's seamless map you also see vendors in neighbouring zones.
- Renamed the tabs to *Arrows/Bullets* and *Quivers/Pouches*.
- The main window can be resized with the grip in its bottom right corner. Its size is saved.
- Fixed: vendor pins missing on the default world map. They now use the game's own map pin system, so they work with the default map, OpenMap and other map addons.

## 1.3.0
- New: ammo vendors on the world map and the minimap, each can be turned on or off.
- New: auto-buy the best arrows or bullets for your weapon and level when you talk to a vendor. Only fills your quiver or ammo pouch. Hold Shift to skip.
- New: quiver / ammo pouch slots are detected automatically, or can be set by hand.
- New: the **Arrow/Projectile** and **Quiver/Pouch** tabs list every item with level, stats and how to get it (vendors, quests, drops, crafting, reputation).
- New: two private chat reminders 400 and 200 shots before the raid warning.
- Renamed the tabs *Ammo* and *Bags* to *Arrow/Projectile* and *Quiver/Pouch*.

## 1.2.0
- Redesigned main window in a dark, flat style with a sidebar.
- New tabs: **Ammo** and **Bags** (coming soon) and **Settings**.
- Quick *Hide from minimap* button in the sidebar.
- Addon icon in the AddOns list.

## 1.1.0
- New options window, opened with `/ammo`, the minimap button or a right-click on the ammo counter.
- New minimap button. Drag it around the minimap, or turn it off in the options.
- The ammo counter's shown/hidden state is now saved between sessions.
- `/ammo help` lists all commands; new `/ammo minimap` command.

## 1.0.0
- Initial release.
- Ammo counter with color coding and a movable, lockable frame.
- Low-ammo raid warning with a configurable threshold.
- Support for WoW Forever (interface 16001), Classic Era and TBC.
